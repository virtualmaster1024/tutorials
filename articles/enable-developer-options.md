### Overview: Unlocking Developer Options
Developer Options unlocks system-level Android settings required for Virtual Master:
- **Wireless Debugging**: Connect on-device to grant high-framerate Vulkan permissions without a PC.
- **USB Debugging**: Connect to computers for adb command line testing and automation.
- **Display Refresh Rate**: Monitor 120Hz/90Hz hardware passthrough performance in real time.

---

### Animated Step-by-Step Walkthrough (Google Pixel & Stock Android)

![Enabling Developer Options on Google Pixel](images/enable-dev-options-Google.gif)

Follow the 4 simple steps shown in the GIF above:
1. Open your phone **Settings**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **About phone**.
3. Scroll down to find **Build number**.
4. Tap **Build number** rapidly **7 times** in a row until you see the message: *"You are now a developer!"*.
5. Return to **Settings** -> **System** -> **Developer options** to access the menu.

### Brand-Specific Instructions

#### Samsung Galaxy (One UI 5 / 6 / 7)
1. Open **Settings** -> **About phone** -> **Software information**.
2. Tap **Build number** **7 times**.
3. Enter your lock screen PIN or password.
4. Return to **Settings**; **Developer options** will now appear at the bottom.

#### Xiaomi, Redmi & POCO (MIUI / HyperOS)
1. Open **Settings** -> **About phone**.
2. Tap **OS version** (HyperOS) or **MIUI version** **7 times**.
3. Access via **Settings** -> **Additional settings** -> **Developer options**.

#### OnePlus, OPPO & Realme (ColorOS / OxygenOS)
1. Open **Settings** -> **About device** -> **Version**.
2. Tap **Version number** **7 times**.
3. Access via **Settings** -> **Additional settings** -> **Developer options**.
